HOW TO PLAY

RULES: 1) Drag from a dot to its matching dot. 2) Lines can’t cross or overlap. 3) Fill EVERY cell, not just the dots. 4) Beat the timer. 5) Moves are limited, so plan carefully! 6) Hard mode: 💣 bombs erase your lines. Tap a bomb to defuse it!

What is Flow Matrix?

A color puzzle. Each color has two dots. Your job is to join them with a line.

How to draw

Press a dot, then drag with your mouse or finger through cells that touch side by side. No diagonals. You can’t go through X cells.

When lines meet

Lines can’t share a cell. If you drag over another color’s line, that line is cut and erased. Dragging back over your own line shortens it.

Clear or restart a line

Press its dot again and redraw. Undo takes back your last move. Reset clears the board (it asks first).

Fill the whole board

On every level in every mode, every usable cell must be covered. If all colors are joined but empty cells remain, you will see “Almost there!” and the level is not finished.

Timer

Easy gives you 1 minute, Medium 2 minutes and Hard 3 minutes. It starts at your first drag. It stops when you pause, win or lose. Leaving the tab pauses the game.

Limited moves

Every line you draw counts as one move, and so does a board reset. Undo does not give moves back. The counter at the top shows moves used and moves allowed. When it runs out, you lose. Plan before you draw!

Bombs (Hard mode)

In Hard mode a 💣 can appear on your lines. It has a 3-second countdown and a red danger zone. Tap the bomb to defuse it. If it explodes, it erases your lines inside the red zone and you must redraw them.

Scoring and stars

You earn points for finishing, for time left and for harder boards. Hints, extra moves and retries lower your points. 3 stars need fast, clean play. 1 star means you finished.

Win or lose

You win when all colors are joined and the board is full. You lose when the timer hits 00:00, or when you run out of moves. Then you can try again.

Unlocking

Finish a level to open the next one. Finish Easy to open Medium. Finish Medium to open Hard. Finish all 30 levels to unlock Endless Flow.

Endless Flow

Puzzles never end and get harder each level. Endless uses the same timers (1, 2 or 3 minutes) and limited moves, and Hard Endless has bombs too. Win in a row to build a streak bonus. If the timer runs out, the run is over.
